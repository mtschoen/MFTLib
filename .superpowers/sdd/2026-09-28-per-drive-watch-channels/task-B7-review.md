Also, the required cancellation test is supposed to gate a start and prove no handle remains published (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-28-per-drive-watch-channels-design.md:1362`); here T and U are held before they reach the source, while V deliberately ignores cancellation and publishes a handle, and the assertions exclude V (`MFTLib.Tests/Index/FileIndexBatchedOperationTests.cs:72`, `MFTLib.Tests/Index/FileIndexBatchedOperationTests.cs:79`, `MFTLib.Tests/Index/FileIndexBatchedOperationTests.cs:91`).
